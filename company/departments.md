# Departments and responsibilities

| Function | Responsibilities | Control boundary to model later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finance | Posting policies, payables, receivables, tax, close, consolidation | Separate preparation, approval, and payment release |
| Sales and customer service | B2B terms, customer orders, deposits, returns | Credit and refund approval |
| E-commerce | Shopify catalog, checkout, promotions, payment operations | Order-to-settlement reconciliation |
| Procurement | China sourcing, purchase orders, supplier coordination | Purchasing approval and bill matching |
| Supply chain and logistics | Replenishment, freight, warehouse and 3PL coordination | Ownership/custody and movement reconciliation |
| Quality | Lot release, returns inspection, expiry and quarantine | Saleable inventory release |
| Business systems | Master data, integrations, access, monitoring, recovery | Change approval and auditability |

Small teams may share people, but conflicting permissions and independent review still need explicit treatment. Organizational functions are not legal entities.
