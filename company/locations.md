| TRANSIT | Logical inventory-in-transit location | Carrier identified per shipment | Entity identified per shipment |

Track location separately from legal owner. Transit records need shipment, origin, destination, owner, carrier, title-transfer evidence, and dates. Warehouses require saleable, quarantine, damaged, and returns states; these are stock states, not extra legal entities.
