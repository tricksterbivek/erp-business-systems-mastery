# Tax jurisdiction scope

| Jurisdiction | Simulation scope | Facts required before calculating |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Australia | ABG-AU assumed GST-registered; domestic GST and cross-border cases | Supply type, location, registration, tax date, invoice evidence |
| New Zealand | ABG-NZ assumed GST-registered | Supply type, location, registration, tax date, import/export evidence |
| United States | ABG-US sales tax across selected states | Nexus, ship-to jurisdiction, product classification, exemption evidence, effective date |
| European Union | ABG-AU assumed registered for VAT in Netherlands; initial EU operations centered there | Place of supply, customer status, VAT IDs, movement of goods, importer, effective date |

This is a scope model, not a tax engine. Rates, thresholds, filing obligations, import tax recovery, and any additional EU registrations must be sourced and dated when a problem needs them. Do not presume one US rate or one EU VAT treatment, automatic exemption for exports, or that a VAT registration resolves every local obligation. China manufacturing is a sourcing context; detailed China tax compliance is deferred.
