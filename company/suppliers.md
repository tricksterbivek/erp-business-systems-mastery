| LOG-NZ | Harbour Fulfilment Services | NZ 3PL | NZD |
| LOG-EU | Delta Fulfilment Services | Netherlands 3PL | EUR |

ABG-AU is the initial buyer from China and freight coordinator. Receiving, billing, and payment are separate events. Subsidiaries contract for local 3PL services, while ABG-AU contracts for EU services. Group entities become intercompany counterparties when buying from one another; do not treat independent suppliers as group entities.
