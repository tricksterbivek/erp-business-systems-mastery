# Shared glossary

A small starting vocabulary. Add terms when they become relevant; avoid turning this into a textbook.

| Term | Working meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Legal entity | Company that owns rights, obligations, books, and transactions |
| Subsidiary | Legal entity controlled by the parent |
| ERP transaction | Recorded business event with defined state changes and downstream effects |
| Subledger | Detailed records supporting a general-ledger control balance |
| General ledger | Accounts and postings used to produce financial statements |
| Customer deposit | Cash received before the related obligation is satisfied; track separately from earned revenue |
| Vendor bill | Supplier claim for payment, distinct from a purchase order or goods receipt |
| Inventory in transit | Owned goods moving between locations, with custody separate from ownership |
| Landed cost | Eligible acquisition costs allocated to inventory under the chosen policy |
| 3PL | Third-party logistics provider holding or moving goods on behalf of an owner |
| Functional currency | Currency of an entity's primary economic environment |
| Consolidation | Combining group entities with translation and elimination adjustments |
| Intercompany | Transaction or balance between group legal entities |
| GST / VAT | Consumption taxes whose treatment depends on jurisdiction and transaction facts |
| US sales tax | Transaction tax requiring jurisdiction, nexus, product, and customer facts |
| Idempotency | Repeated processing of one event produces no additional business effect |
| Reconciliation | Explaining and resolving differences between independently derived records |
