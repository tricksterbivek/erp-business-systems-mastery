## Assessment boundary and help received

Selected Level 1 classifications and basic calculations are supported. Level 2 across the module is not demonstrated: no complete nine-step walkthrough exists. Level 3 is not awarded merely for correct arithmetic or simple journal entries.

Tutor assistance included concepts, analogous examples, explicit corrected entries, and answer explanations. Independent fresh answers are distinguished above from corrections following help. Some earlier tutor responses supplied solutions too quickly; those responses are assistance, not independent learner evidence.

Observed misconceptions: debit/credit treated as universal plus/minus; revenue confused with cash collection; credit memo confused with refund; principal confused with expense; balancing applied to each account rather than the entire entry; depreciation confused with cash outflow.
